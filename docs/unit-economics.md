# Unit Economics — Terra Hearth Open Bakery

## Target Benchmarks (Mature Operation)

| Metric                     | Target          | Notes |
|----------------------------|-----------------|-------|
| Gross Margin               | 60–70%          | 65%+ on best SKUs |
| COGS (Organic Ingredients) | 28–35%          | Hardest lever to control |
| Labour                     | 25–32%          | Lower in pure cloud model |
| Occupancy (Cloud Kitchen)  | 8–12%           | Critical to keep low |
| Packaging                  | 4–7%            | Eco packaging costs more |
| Platform / Delivery Fees   | 0–25%           | Push own-channel hard |
| Marketing                  | 3–6%            | Content-led is efficient |
| Food Waste                 | < 3%            | Non-negotiable |
| Mature EBITDA              | 12–18%          | 15%+ is strong |

## Why Organic Economics Are Different

Organic flours and ingredients typically cost 25–50%+ more than conventional. This premium must be recovered through:
1. Higher selling prices (premium positioning)
2. Extremely low waste
3. High subscription / direct sales mix (lower platform fees)
4. High-margin pantry items made from discard and day-olds
5. Efficient labour and low occupancy

If waste exceeds 5–6%, organic economics become very difficult.

## Cloud Kitchen vs Retail Economics (Indicative)

| Factor              | Cloud / Micro Kitchen | Full Retail / Café |
|---------------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| Startup Capital     | ₹3–8 lakh            | ₹15–40+ lakh      |
| Monthly Fixed Cost  | Lower                 | Much higher        |
| Break-even Speed    | Faster (6–14 months)  | Slower             |
| Risk Level          | Medium-Low            | High               |
| Margin Potential    | Higher early          | Lower until scale  |

**Recommendation**: Master cloud + subscription economics before opening a customer-facing café.

## Pricing Philosophy

Price for value and integrity, not volume. Customers who care about true organic and long fermentation will pay a premium. Those who only want “brown bread” are not your target.

## Continuous Monitoring

Track weekly:
- Food cost % by SKU
- Waste % (by weight and by value)
- Contribution margin after platform fees
- Subscription retention and average order value
- Labour hours per kg of finished product

Adjust recipes, pricing, and production volume ruthlessly based on data.
