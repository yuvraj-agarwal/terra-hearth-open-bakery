# Certification Guide — India (NPOP + FSSAI)

This guide covers the minimum requirements to legally sell products labelled “Organic” in India as of 2026.

## 1. FSSAI Licence (Mandatory for any food business)

| Turnover                  | Licence Type          | Form   | Approx. Cost (indicative) |
|---------------------------|-----------------------|--------|---------------------------|
| Up to ₹12 lakh            | Basic Registration    | Form A | Very low                  |
| ₹12 lakh – ₹20 crore      | State Licence         | Form B | Moderate                  |
| Above ₹20 crore or multi-state | Central Licence  | Form B | Higher                    |

Apply at: https://foodlicensing.fssai.gov.in

You need this **before** or in parallel with organic certification.

## 2. Organic Certification — NPOP (Recommended)

**National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP)** administered by APEDA.

- Gives the right to use the **India Organic** logo
- Required for credible “organic” claims on multi-ingredient bakery products
- Preferred by premium retailers and for future export

### Process Overview

1. Choose an APEDA-accredited Certification Body (CB)
   Examples: Ecocert, Control Union, Bureau Veritas, OneCert, IMO, Lacon, etc.
2. Submit application + Organic System Plan for your processing unit
3. On-site inspection
4. Evaluation and issuance of Scope Certificate
5. Annual surveillance

### Approximate Costs (Processor Category, 2025–26 range)

- On-site inspection: ₹12,000 – ₹20,000 per man-day
- Certification fee: ₹15,000 – ₹25,000
- Label assessment: ₹1,000 – ₹2,000 per product
- Travel & testing: actuals
- First-year total for a small bakery processor: typically ₹40,000 – ₹1,20,000 depending on complexity and CB

Always get written quotes from at least two CBs.

### Key Rules for Bakeries

- ≥95% organic ingredients required for multi-ingredient products to be labelled “Certified Organic”
- Full traceability from farm to finished product
- Prevention of contamination / co-mingling with non-organic
- Only allowed non-agricultural processing aids may be used
- Maintain detailed records for audits

## 3. PGS-India Alternative

Participatory Guarantee System — cheaper and suitable for very small direct-to-consumer operations, but weaker brand signal and not accepted for export or many premium retail channels. Most serious bakeries choose NPOP.

## 4. Practical Timeline

| Activity                        | Typical Duration      |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------|
| FSSAI application & grant       | 2–8 weeks             |
| NPOP application to certificate | 2–4 months            |
| Total before legitimate organic launch | 3–5 months       |

Start both processes early. Do not sell anything labelled “organic” until properly certified.

## 5. Ongoing Obligations

- Annual renewal / surveillance audit
- Transaction certificates when required
- Immediate reporting of major changes in ingredients or processes
- Proper logo usage on packaging (India Organic + FSSAI)

---

**Always verify current rules** on APEDA and FSSAI websites. Regulations and fees change.
