# Product Matrix — Terra Hearth Open Bakery

## Core Philosophy
Every product must satisfy **all** of the following:
- 100% organic ingredients (NPOP / equivalent)
- No commercial yeast (wild fermentation or natural leavening only)
- No refined sugar
- No synthetic dough conditioners, emulsifiers, or preservatives
- Designed for maximum nutrient density and digestibility

## Launch Recommendation

**Start with only 6–8 SKUs**:
1. Classic Multigrain Sourdough
2. Ragi-Jowar Boule
3. One seeded heirloom wheat loaf
4. One GF millet loaf
5. Discard Crackers
6. One functional pastry (Turmeric-Jaggery rolls or similar)
7. Spiced Millet Granola
8. One cookie / rusk

Expand only after demand and production consistency are proven.
