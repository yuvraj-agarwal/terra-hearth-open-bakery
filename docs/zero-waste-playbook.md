# Zero-Waste Playbook

Waste is the silent killer of organic bakery margins. Organic ingredients already carry a 25–50%+ premium. Any significant waste destroys the model.

## Core Rule
**Design the production system so that almost nothing is thrown away.**

## The Loop

### 1. Day-Old Bread
- Convert into:
  - Artisan crostini / bruschetta bases
  - Panko-style breadcrumbs
  - Seeded sourdough crackers
  - Bread pudding (limited, high-margin)
- Sell as pantry items or B2B to restaurants/cafés

### 2. Sourdough Discard / Starter
- Use daily in:
  - Pancake / waffle batters
  - Cracker doughs
  - Cookie or scone batters
  - Flatbreads
- Never pour large quantities down the drain

### 3. Trimmings & Failed Batches
- Salvageable → rework into crackers or breadcrumbs
- Unsalvageable food waste → local organic farm compost or biogas partner
- Track weight of food waste weekly and set reduction targets

### 4. Packaging
- Use only compostable, recyclable, or reusable packaging
- Avoid plastic wherever possible
- Educate customers on how to dispose of packaging correctly

## Operational Practices

- Bake to demand as much as possible (subscriptions help enormously)
- Produce high-margin “waste products” as intentional SKUs, not afterthoughts
- Train every team member on the zero-waste system
- Measure waste as a % of production by weight and by value every week
- Target: < 3% total food waste

## Why This Matters Financially

If organic flour costs 40% more and you waste 8% instead of 2%, the effective cost increase becomes extreme. Zero-waste is not just environmental — it is core unit economics for any serious organic bakery.
