# Terra Hearth Open Bakery

**Open-source business model & operational playbook for a certified organic, gut-first sourdough bakery.**

> Build a high-integrity organic bakery that prioritizes nutrition, fermentation science, zero waste, and real organic certification — starting lean and scaling intelligently.

This playbook is designed primarily for the Indian market (NPOP + FSSAI) but is adaptable worldwide. It is released under the MIT License so founders, bakers, and operators can freely use, modify, and commercialize it.

---

## Why This Exists

Most bakery startups fail because of:
- High fixed costs (premature retail/café)
- Weak differentiation (just “brown bread”)
- Poor unit economics on organic ingredients
- Regulatory missteps on organic claims
- High food waste

Terra Hearth solves these with a **cloud-first + subscription-heavy + zero-waste + rigorous organic** model.

---

## Core Principles

1. **True Organic Only** — NPOP (India Organic) + FSSAI compliance. No “natural” or “clean-label” loopholes.
2. **Long Fermentation** — 24–48 hour cold-proofed wild sourdough. No commercial yeast.
3. **Indian Ancient Grains First** — Millets (ragi, jowar, bajra, amaranth, foxtail) + selective heirloom wheat/spelt.
4. **Zero Refined Sugar** — Jaggery, date paste, coconut sugar, limited monk fruit or raw honey.
5. **Zero Waste by Design** — Day-old bread → crackers/crostini; discard → batters; scraps → compost.
6. **Predictable Cash Flow** — Heavy emphasis on subscriptions and B2B before expensive retail.
7. **Radical Transparency** — QR codes to farm/batch data.

## Recommended Launch Path (India)

| Phase | Timeline       | Focus                              | Capital Intensity | Primary Channels                  |
|-------|----------------|------------------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 1     | 0–9 months     | Cloud / Micro kitchen              | Low               | Own site + aggregators + subscription waitlist |
| 2     | 6–15 months    | Subscription + B2B                 | Medium            | Bread Club + organic stores/hotels |
| 3     | 12–24 months   | Par-baked + limited retail         | Medium–High       | Regional shipping + small counter  |
| 4     | 18+ months     | Flagship café / multi-kitchen      | High              | Only after proven unit economics  |

**Do not open a flagship café first.** Rent and front-of-house labour kill most early bakery startups in India.

## Certification & Compliance (India)

- **FSSAI licence** (State or Central depending on turnover)
- **NPOP certification** for the processing facility (mandatory for “organic” claims)
- Multi-ingredient products must contain ≥95% organic ingredients to use the full “Certified Organic” claim
- Maintain full traceability and prevent cross-contamination (especially gluten-free lines)

See `docs/certification-guide-india.md` for step-by-step process and approximate costs.

|-------------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------|

| COGS (Organic Ingredients)    | 28–35%           | Requires direct supplier relationships     |
| Labour                        | 25–32%           | Lower in pure cloud model                  |
| Occupancy (Cloud)             | 8–12%            | Keep rent low                              |
| Food Waste                    | < 3%             | Critical for organic economics             |
| Mature Net / EBITDA           | 12–18%           | 15%+ is excellent                          |

## Zero-Waste Loop (Non-Negotiable)

1. Day-old bread → artisan crostini, panko, seeded crackers
2. Sourdough discard → pancakes, batters, snack doughs
3. Inedible scraps → local organic farm compost or biogas
4. Packaging → compostable / recyclable only

This system protects margins when organic ingredients cost 25–50% more.
